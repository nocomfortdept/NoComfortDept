## Headline Formulas

### Formula 1 — The Spec Lead
`[Material Spec] + [Identity Implication]`
- "400gsm. Built for men who don't explain themselves."
- "240gsm jersey. Shoulder-forward. Period."

### Formula 2 — The Anti-Statement
`Not [what NCD isn't] + [what it is]`
- "Not hype. Heavyweight."
- "Not for everyone. For you."
- "Not comfortable. Committed."

### Formula 3 — The Earned Claim
`[Verb that implies process] + [Outcome]`
- "Built to outlast."
- "Forged to last years, not seasons."
- "Cut for the long game."

### Formula 4 — The Drop Frame
`Drop [#] + [Scarcity signal]`
- "Drop 5. [#] units. No restock."
- "Drop 4 is live. While it is."

### Formula 5 — The Identity Declaration
`[Who NCD is for] — stated as truth`
- "For men who already decided."
- "Worn by the ones who showed up."
- "The standard for men who hold one."

### Formula 6 — The Contrast
`[Mass market behavior] vs. [NCD behavior]`
- "Everyone else dropped price. We dropped a new hoodie."
- "They followed the trend. You set the standard."
