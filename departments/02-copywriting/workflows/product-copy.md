# Workflow: Product Copy
## Department: Copywriting

Use this workflow every time a new product needs copy — from tagline through full description.

---

## Inputs Required

Before starting, confirm you have:
- [ ] Product name
- [ ] Category (hoodie / tee / jogger / shorts / accessory)
- [ ] Key materials and specs (weight, fabric blend, construction details)
- [ ] Colorway(s)
- [ ] Drop number (for drop-framing)
- [ ] Price point
- [ ] Inventory quantity (for scarcity calibration)
- [ ] Campaign narrative from brand-strategy (if applicable)

---

## Step 1 — Tagline (1 line)

Write 3 tagline options. Choose the one that passes: *"Would a high-standard man say this about himself?"*

**Formula:** [Specific material/construction fact] + [Identity implication]

Examples:
- "400gsm of shut-the-hell-up-and-work."
- "Not your warmup. Your standard."
- "Built for the ones who showed up."

**Deliver:** 1 final tagline + 2 backup options

---

## Step 2 — Product Description (3–6 sentences)

**Sentence 1:** Lead with construction or material specificity. Never lead with adjectives.
**Sentence 2:** What the construction means — implied quality signal.
**Sentence 3:** What wearing this says about the man (identity, not aspiration).
**Sentence 4 (optional):** The one thing that separates this product from everything else.
**Sentence 5–6 (optional):** Close with earned authority, never a plea.

**Template:**
```
[Weight]gsm [fabric]. [Construction detail — seams, wash, structure].
[Structural implication — holds shape / moves with you / built to outlast].
The [Product Name] is for [specific man description — not everyone].
[Differentiator — what makes this the choice].
[Identity close — who they are when they wear it].
```

---

## Step 3 — Features List (3–5 bullet points)

Format: pipe-delimited for Shopify metafield injection.

```
[Spec 1] | [Spec 2] | [Spec 3] | [Spec 4] | [Spec 5]
```

Examples:
- `400gsm heavyweight cotton blend` | `Reinforced double-needle stitching` | `Pre-shrunk for consistent sizing` | `Garment-washed for immediate shape` | `Shoulder-forward masculine cut`

---

## Step 4 — SEO Meta Description (max 155 chars)

Must include: brand name, product name, one key spec, one identity word.
Never: exclamation marks, "buy now", discount language.

---

## Step 5 — Size Guide Copy

Write one sentence per size describing the fit. Practical, not aspirational.

---

## Step 6 — Shipping/Returns Copy

Standard NCD template (update if policy changes):
> Ships within 24 hours. Free returns within 30 days — no questions. Exchanges always free. We stand behind every piece.

---

## Step 7 — Quality Gate

Run the brand voice checklist before delivering:
- [ ] No passive voice
- [ ] No banned words
- [ ] At least one specific material stat
- [ ] Speaks to one man, not all men
- [ ] Last line lands without explanation

---

## Delivery Format

```
PRODUCT COPY DELIVERABLE
Product: [Name]
Drop: [#]

TAGLINE: [Final tagline]
TAGLINE ALT 1: [Option]
TAGLINE ALT 2: [Option]

DESCRIPTION:
[Full description]

FEATURES:
[Feature 1] | [Feature 2] | [Feature 3]

META DESCRIPTION:
[155-char max]

SHIPPING COPY: [Standard or custom]
```
