# Workflow: Email Copy
## Department: Copywriting → Retention & Email

Use this workflow when `07-retention-email` provides a flow structure requiring body copy.

---

## NCD Email Voice Rules

1. Subject lines are statements or questions — never commands
2. Preheader earns the open — treat it like a second subject line
3. Body copy never exceeds what's needed — NCD emails are short
4. One link per email — never scatter attention
5. Sign-off is always NCD, never "The Team" or "We"

---

## Email Copy Templates

### 1. Welcome Email (Flow: Email 1)

**Subject:** You're in.
**Preheader:** Here's what NoComfortDept actually is.

**Body:**
```
You just joined something that isn't for everyone.

NoComfortDept was built for men who hold themselves to a standard
most people don't understand.

This isn't hype. It's not fast fashion.
It's heavyweight clothing made for the long game.

Here's what to expect:
→ Limited drops. No restocks.
→ Every piece built to outlast the trend.
→ A community that shows up.

Your first drop access is below.

[CTA: Shop Drop [#]]

NCD
```

---

### 2. Drop Announcement (Flow: Campaign)

**Subject:** Drop [#] is live.
**Preheader:** [#] units. That's all there is.

**Body:**
```
Drop [#] is here.

[Product Name] — [Key spec, e.g., 400gsm heavyweight, shoulder-forward cut].

[1–2 sentences of product copy — use product-copy workflow output]

[Unit count] units. No restock after sellout.

[CTA: Shop Drop [#] →]

NCD
```

---

### 3. Abandoned Cart (Flow: Email 1 — 1hr after abandonment)

**Subject:** Still there.
**Preheader:** [#] units left in your size.

**Body:**
```
Your [Product Name] is still in your cart.

[Inventory count] left in [size]. After that, this colorway is archived.

We don't do "hurry up" tactics. But we do tell the truth.

[CTA: Complete Your Order →]

NCD
```

---

### 4. Abandoned Cart (Flow: Email 2 — 24hr)

**Subject:** This is the last email.
**Preheader:** We're not going to keep asking.

**Body:**
```
We're not going to keep asking.

If the [Product Name] is right for you, you already know it.

If it's not — no hard feelings.

But if you're still thinking about it: [#] units left. Ships tomorrow.

[CTA: Finish What You Started →]

NCD
```

---

### 5. Post-Purchase (Flow: Email 1 — Order confirmation supplement)

**Subject:** It's on its way.
**Preheader:** You made a good call.

**Body:**
```
Your order is confirmed.

[Product Name] ships within 24 hours.
You'll get tracking when it's out the door.

No buyer's remorse needed — you chose right.

While you wait: [link to other drop or collection]

NCD
```

---

### 6. Win-Back (Flow: Email 1 — 60 days inactive)

**Subject:** We kept building.
**Preheader:** Drop [#] dropped while you were gone.

**Body:**
```
It's been a minute.

We didn't stop. Drop [#] dropped.
New colorways. Same standards. Limited units.

If you're ready: [link to current drop]

If not — no pressure. The work continues regardless.

NCD
```
