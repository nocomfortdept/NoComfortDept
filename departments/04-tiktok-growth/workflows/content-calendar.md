# Workflow: Content Calendar
## Department: TikTok Growth

Build and manage the monthly TikTok content calendar here.

---

## Calendar Structure

**Posting frequency:** 5–7 videos per week
**Pillar distribution:**
- Product Truth: 40% (2–3/week)
- Identity Mirror: 30% (2/week)
- Drop Mechanics: 20% (1–2/week, increase in drop week)
- Social Proof: 10% (1/week)

---

## Weekly Rhythm Template

| Day | Pillar | Content Type | Hook Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Identity Mirror | Motivational / dark morning aesthetic | Text hook: "The standard." |
| Tuesday | Product Truth | Fabric detail / macro texture | Visual hook: extreme close-up |
| Wednesday | Drop Mechanics | Drop countdown / teaser | Text hook: "X days." |
| Thursday | Social Proof | UGC fit check or customer reaction | Text hook: "Rating this honestly:" |
| Friday | Product Truth | Full outfit reveal / styling | Visual hook: dramatic reveal |
| Saturday | Identity Mirror | Training / discipline content | Voice hook |
| Sunday | Drop Mechanics | Behind the scenes or archive content | Text hook |

---

## Drop Week Calendar (intensified)

| Day | Content | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| D-7 | "Something is dropping." — no product, just tension | Build anticipation |
| D-5 | Fabric detail / production teaser | Qualify interest |
| D-3 | "3 days." — countdown, unit count teaser | FOMO build |
| D-1 | "Tomorrow." — hero shot, no details | Maximum anticipation |
| D-0 (AM) | Drop live announcement — product reveal | Drive link clicks |
| D-0 (PM) | Repost / duet if customers post fits | Social proof |
| D+1 | Stock update — "X units left" | Urgency close |
| D+2 (if stock remaining) | Final push — "Last ones" | Close stragglers |
| Sold out | "Gone. Archive." — brand respect + wait list tease | Retention + anticipation |
