# Workflow: TikTok Landing Page Brief
## Department: TikTok Growth → Shopify CRO

Use this to brief a TikTok-specific landing page to `03-shopify-cro` for every major drop or campaign that has dedicated TikTok traffic.

---

## When to Request a Landing Page

- A video is expected to drive significant link-in-bio traffic
- A paid TikTok campaign is launching with a specific product angle
- A drop is launching and needs a dedicated conversion surface
- An existing LP needs updating for a new colorway or hook angle

---

---

## After Brief is Submitted

1. Send completed brief to CRO Specialist
2. CRO builds using `departments/03-shopify-cro/workflows/lp-brief.md`
3. CRO returns live URL + preview link
4. TikTok Growth tests on mobile before updating link in bio
5. Add UTM link to TikTok bio
6. Monitor: link clicks, CVR, ATC rate for first 48 hours
