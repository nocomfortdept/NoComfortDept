# Workflow: Hook Writing
## Department: TikTok Growth

The hook is the entire game. If the first 2 seconds don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters.

---

## The Hook Framework

A TikTok hook has three layers:

1. **Visual Hook** — What the viewer sees in frame 1
2. **Text Hook** — On-screen text in the first 2 seconds (if used)
3. **Audio Hook** — First spoken word or sound hit (if voiceover)

All three must be present or deliberately absent. No accidents.

---

## Hook Writing Process

### Step 1 — Identify the Core Tension

Every great hook creates a tension that demands resolution. What does the viewer *need* to know after seeing your opening?

NCD tension categories:
- **Identity tension:** "Is this for me?" → hooks that make the right man feel called out
- **Quality tension:** "What IS that?" → hooks that lead with a material detail too specific to ignore
- **FOMO tension:** "What did I miss?" → hooks that imply something already happened
- **Contrast tension:** "Wait, that's not what I expected." → hooks that subvert expectations

---

### Step 2 — Write 5 Hook Variants

For each piece of content, write 5 hook options across different tension types:

```
Hook A (Identity): ___
Hook B (Quality):  ___
Hook C (FOMO):     ___
Hook D (Contrast): ___
Hook E (Direct):   ___
```

Choose the one with the highest specificity and lowest explanation required.

---

### Step 3 — The 2-Second Test

Read your hook aloud. Stop at 2 seconds. Ask:
- Does someone who doesn't know NCD want to keep watching?
- Does it create a question that only the video can answer?
- Would a distracted person on a phone notice this?

If no to any → rewrite.

## Approved Hook Swipe File

### Text Hooks (on-screen, first 2s)
```
"WAIT"
"Watch this."
"This changes how you dress."
"I stopped buying clothes. I started buying this."
"POV: you finally stopped settling."
"400gsm. Let me explain."
"This sold out in 48 hours."
"Not for everyone. Skip if that's you."
"They said it was too expensive. It's $[price]."
"I've worn this every week for 3 months."
"The last hoodie you'll buy."
"Drop [#] is here."
"[#] units left."
"Rated this objectively:"
```

### Voice Hooks (spoken, first 2s)
```
"I need to be honest with you."
"Nobody talks about what quality actually feels like."
"Here's what [price] in clothing actually looks like."
"You've been lied to about what premium means."
"This is what I ordered. This is what arrived."
"If you know, you know. If you don't — watch this."
"Three months of wear. Here's my honest rating."
```

### Visual Hooks (no text)
```
- Fast fabric texture zoom from black → reveal
- Gold light hitting stitching detail — macro
- Single product on dark flat — cinematic reveal
- Hands feeling weight of fabric — sensory
- Model not looking at camera — intentional stillness
- Quick cut: chaos → NCD product → silence
```
