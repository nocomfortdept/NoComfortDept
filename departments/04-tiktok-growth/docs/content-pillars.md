# NoComfortDept — TikTok Content Pillars
## Maintained by: TikTok Growth

---

## The Four Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Product Truth (40%)
**Purpose:** Convert skeptics. Prove quality without claiming it.
**Tone:** Honest, factual, specific. Let the product speak.
**Viewer outcome:** "I didn't know this brand was this serious about quality."

Content formats:
- Fabric weight and texture demonstration
- Construction detail — seams, stitching, hardware
- Side-by-side: NCD vs. fast fashion competitor (never name them)
- Honest wear test — 3 months / 6 months / 1 year of use
- Unboxing with emphasis on packaging quality and presentation

**Don't:**
- Use superlatives without proof ("best quality ever")
- Show product in lifestyle without showing the product first
- Rush the reveal

---

### Pillar 2 — Identity Mirror (30%)
**Purpose:** Build connection. Make the right man feel seen.
**Tone:** Philosophical, earned, quiet. No hype.
**Viewer outcome:** "This brand gets me. It's speaking my language."

Content formats:
- Dark morning training / discipline content with NCD worn naturally
- Intentional man going about his day — NCD as part of the uniform, not the point
- Minimalist environment content — workspace, gym, raw architecture
- Text-driven philosophy clips (no product — brand values only)
- "The man who wears NCD" character studies

**Don't:**
- Make it look like a cologne commercial
- Use stock footage or generic lifestyle
- Feature NCD loudly — it should be present, not performing

---

### Pillar 3 — Drop Mechanics (20%)
**Purpose:** Create urgency. Drive link-in-bio traffic. Build anticipation culture.
**Tone:** Factual, matter-of-fact. No desperation.
**Viewer outcome:** "I need to click the link now."

Content formats:
- Drop countdown series (D-7, D-3, D-1, D-0)
- Behind-the-scenes production footage
- Unit count updates ("47 left")
- Sold-out announcement (respect, not regret)
- Archive content — "This is what sold out in 48 hours"
- Waitlist teaser for next drop

**Don't:**
- Sound desperate — let the scarcity speak
- Inflate urgency with false numbers
- Post drop mechanics content outside of drop windows (keep it rare)

---

### Pillar 4 — Social Proof (10%)
**Purpose:** Validate through community. Convert the fence-sitters.
**Tone:** Community-feel, honest, unscripted.
**Viewer outcome:** "Real people buy this. Real people love this."

Content formats:
- Customer fit check reposts / duets
- UGC reaction clips with NCD commentary
- Review-style content from loyal customers
- Community callouts ("Tag @nocomfortdept in your fit")
- "X people ordered this in the last 24 hours" data content

**Don't:**
- Use obviously scripted testimonials
- Pay influencers for inauthentic reviews
- Feature customers who don't represent the NCD man

---

## Cross-Pillar Rules

1. Every video has a hook that earns the next 5 seconds
2. Every video has a reason to click the link — even if it's subtle
3. No video posts without a caption built from `workflows/hook-writing.md`
4. No video posts without correct UTM on link-in-bio
5. Drop Mechanics content only runs in drop windows — never generic urgency
