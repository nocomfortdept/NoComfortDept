## Relationships by Department

### → 02 Copywriting (Primary collaboration)

**What Brand Strategy gives Copywriting:**
- Campaign narrative briefs (before every drop)
- Voice calibration memos (when copy drifts)
- Approved word bank and banned word list
- Seasonal tone direction

**What Brand Strategy receives from Copywriting:**
- All copy for approval (product pages, emails, ads, captions)
- Requests for clarification on voice in new contexts
- Weekly copy output summary

**SLA:** Brand Strategy responds to copy submissions within 4 hours.

---

### → 05 Creative Direction (Secondary collaboration)

**What Brand Strategy gives Creative Direction:**
- Campaign narrative brief (which drives moodboard)
- Seasonal color/mood direction
- Visual identity boundaries (from brand bible section 4)
- Approval/rejection reasoning on assets

**What Brand Strategy receives from Creative Direction:**
- Moodboards for review before shoots
- Asset reviews requesting brand alignment check
- Questions about whether a new visual direction fits brand

**SLA:** Review moodboards within 24h. Review assets within 4h.

---

### → 04 TikTok Growth (Content alignment)

**What Brand Strategy gives TikTok Growth:**
- Content pillar frameworks (done — see `04-tiktok-growth/docs/content-pillars.md`)
- Drop campaign narrative (drives content calendar angle)
- Decisions on trending audio/format fit
- Permission or veto on any new content format

**What Brand Strategy receives from TikTok Growth:**
- Requests for trend approvals
- Weekly content calendar for brand review
- Performance data (which content resonates — feeds back into brand understanding)

---

### → 06 Paid Ads (Message alignment)

**What Brand Strategy gives Paid Ads:**
- Approved ad copy angles per campaign
- Veto on any ad creative that violates brand standards
- Seasonal campaign angle when relevant

**What Brand Strategy receives from Paid Ads:**
- Weekly performance data (which brand angles convert)
- Requests for new copy angle direction
- Alerts when ad ROAS drops (may indicate brand/offer misalignment)

---

### → 07 Retention & Email (Tone alignment)

**What Brand Strategy gives Retention:**
- Subject line tone guidelines (same brand voice as all other channels)
- Seasonal narrative to use in campaign sends
- Approval of any new email series that introduces new brand language

---

### → 03 Shopify CRO (Occasional)

**Collaboration trigger:** CRO needs copy/message direction for UX text (size guide, error messages, trust signals).
**Response:** Brand Strategy provides copy direction; CRO implements.

## Communication Protocols

### How to Request a Brand Decision

Any department can escalate a brand question using this format:

```
BRAND DECISION REQUEST
From: [Department]
Priority: [P1 / P2 / P3]
Question: [Specific decision needed]
Options considered:
  A: ___
  B: ___
Context: [Why this matters now]
Deadline: [When decision is needed]
```

Send to Brand Strategy. Response guaranteed within SLA.

### Receiving a Voice Calibration Memo

When you receive a calibration memo:
1. Read it fully — understand the WHY, not just the what
2. Apply correction to the flagged piece within 2 hours
3. Resubmit for approval if the piece is live or going live
4. Add the rule to your own reference to prevent recurrence
5. Do not argue the correction via message — request a sync if you disagree
