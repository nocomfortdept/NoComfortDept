# Workflow: Voice Calibration
## Department: Brand Strategy → Copywriting

Use this workflow to recalibrate brand voice whenever copy output drifts, a new copywriter is onboarded, or a new channel is introduced.

---

## The NCD Voice in One Sentence

> "A man who has decided. Said plainly. Without apology."

---

## Voice Spectrum

NCD lives at these coordinates:

```
SOFT ◄────────────────────────► HARD
              [NCD]

LOUD ◄────────────────────────► QUIET
                         [NCD]

HYPE ◄────────────────────────► EARNED
                        [NCD]

MASS ◄────────────────────────► SPECIFIC
                          [NCD]
```

NCD is hard but quiet. Earned and specific. Never soft, never loud.

---

## Voice Calibration Exercises

### Exercise 1 — The Rewrite Test

Take this generic copy and make it NCD:

**Generic:** "Our hoodies are premium quality and super comfortable. Perfect for any occasion, you'll love how they feel."

**NCD version should:**
- Remove "comfortable" and "premium"
- Lead with construction or weight
- Speak to identity, not occasion
- End with something earned, not promised

**NCD rewrite example:** "400gsm. Structured. Pre-washed for shape that holds. The Blackout Hoodie doesn't ask what the occasion is. Neither do you."

---

### Exercise 2 — The Identity Test

Every piece of NCD copy must answer: *What does this say about the man who wears it?*

If the copy doesn't answer that question, it's not ready.

---

### Exercise 3 — The Cut Test

Read the copy aloud. Remove every word that doesn't earn its place. If you'd write it for any other brand, cut it. NCD copy should be un-plagiarisable.

---

## Calibration Tiers

| Tier | Symptoms | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 — Minor drift | One or two soft words slipped in | Word-level swap, no rewrite |
| Tier 2 — Voice mismatch | Whole paragraph sounds generic | Paragraph rewrite with identity lens |
| Tier 3 — Brand break | Entire asset sounds like a different brand | Full rewrite + brand audit trigger |

---

## Calibration Memo Format

When issuing a calibration memo to Copywriting:

```
VOICE CALIBRATION MEMO
From: Brand Strategy
To: Copywriting
Date: [DATE]
Tier: [1 / 2 / 3]

ISSUE: [Specific copy that failed]
REASON: [Why it's off-brand]
CORRECTION: [The NCD version]
RULE ADDED: [Any new guidance for brand-bible]
```
