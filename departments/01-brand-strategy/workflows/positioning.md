# Workflow: Market Positioning
## Department: Brand Strategy

Use this workflow when entering a new product category, responding to a new competitor, or refreshing seasonal positioning.

---

## Step 1 — Category Mapping

Define the new category or competitive threat:
- Product/category: ___
- Existing NCD products in this space: ___
- Primary competitors in this space: ___

---

## Step 2 — Competitive Landscape Grid

Map competitors on two axes:

**Axis A:** Mass Market ←→ Limited / Exclusive
**Axis B:** Fashion-Forward ←→ Identity-Driven

Place each competitor. Place NCD last. If NCD lands on top of another brand, the positioning needs to move.

---

## Step 3 — Whitespace Identification

List the unoccupied quadrant NCD should claim:
- What no competitor is saying: ___
- What the customer feels but no brand is reflecting back at them: ___
- The one thing NCD can say that no one else can truthfully say: ___

---

## Step 4 — Positioning Statement

Fill in:
> For [target man] who [belief/behavior], NoComfortDept is the [category] that [key benefit] because [reason to believe].

NCD example:
> For men in their 20s–35s who treat self-improvement as a lifestyle, NoComfortDept is the heavyweight streetwear brand that reflects who they're becoming — not who they're performing as — because every piece is built with the same standards they apply to themselves.

---

## Step 5 — Implications

| Department | What this positioning means for them |
|---|---|
| Copywriting | Lead message, hero copy angle |
| Creative Direction | Visual mood, color story, model direction |
| TikTok Growth | Content hook angle, target persona |
| Paid Ads | Audience targeting, primary message |
| Retention | How to frame the brand in email sequences |

---

## Step 6 — Approval & Distribution

- Document final positioning statement in `docs/brand-bible.md`
- Issue positioning brief to all affected departments
- Set a 30-day check-in to review if positioning is landing
