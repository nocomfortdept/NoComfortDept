# NoComfortDept — Visual Standards
## Maintained by: Creative Direction

The definitive reference for what NCD looks like. Every asset — photo, video, graphic — is measured against this document.

---

## The Visual Identity in One Sentence

> Dark, heavy, intentional. Gold light on black. The man is present but not performing.

---

## Color Rules

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Background | Black or off-black only (`#0a0a0a` or `#111111`) |
| Accent color | Gold `#b8973a` — single directional light source only |
| Urgency / alert | Deep red `#c0392b` — digital UI only (countdowns, badges) — never in photography |
| Text overlay | Off-white `#f5f5f0` — never pure white `#ffffff` in creative |
| Any other color | Must be pre-approved by Creative Director |

**The 20% Rule:** No asset should have more than 20% non-dark tonality. If a background is bright — reshoot or regrade.

---

## Typography in Creative

| Use | Typeface | Weight | Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| On-screen display text | Bebas Neue | Regular | UPPERCASE |
| On-screen body text | Inter | 400 | Sentence case |
| Price / code labels | JetBrains Mono | 400/700 | UPPERCASE |
| Logo | NCD wordmark only | — | UPPERCASE |

**Never:** System fonts, display fonts with serifs, decorative scripts, outlined text without brand approval.

## Photography Standards

### Must Have
- [ ] Directional single light source (gold-warm or steel-cold)
- [ ] Deep shadow with crushed blacks in post
- [ ] Model in intentional posture — never awkward or over-posed
- [ ] Product as clear subject — no composition confusion
- [ ] Clean background — raw material, concrete, black studio

### Must Not Have
- [ ] Bright background (sunlight, white studio, greenery)
- [ ] Smiling model
- [ ] Visible competitor brand logos
- [ ] Generic stock photography feel
- [ ] Retouching that removes skin texture entirely (looks fake)
- [ ] Trendy filters or effects that will date in 12 months

---

## Video Standards

### Must Have
- [ ] Crushed blacks in color grade
- [ ] Single warm or cool accent — consistent throughout
- [ ] Intentional camera movement (or deliberately static)
- [ ] Audio that matches the brand (dark, minimal, or no music)
- [ ] Cuts that are purposeful — no filler footage

### Must Not Have
- [ ] EDM or pop music
- [ ] Handheld shake without intention
- [ ] Bright lifestyle color grading
- [ ] Generic workout video feel
- [ ] Jump cuts mid-sentence
- [ ] Trending audio that conflicts with brand aesthetic
