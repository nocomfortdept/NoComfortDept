# Creative Direction — Daily & Weekly Schedule
## Department: 05 | Employee: Creative Director

---

## Main Objective

Make NCD look like NCD. Set and enforce the visual standard that makes every asset — from a TikTok thumbnail to a product photo — unmistakably NCD without the logo. Brief every shoot, approve every asset, and ensure visual consistency compounds into brand recognition over time.

**North star metric:** Asset approval rate on first pass (target: ≥ 80%). Every rejection is a brief failure, not a photographer failure.

---

## Daily Responsibilities

### Every Day (20–30 min)

| Time | Task | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Review any submitted assets from photographers/editors | Approval, revision, or rejection |
| Morning | Check if TikTok Growth has content going live today — does it meet visual standard? | Pass or flag before posting |
| Ongoing | Answer creative questions from TikTok Growth or Paid Ads | Creative direction decision |
| EOD | Update asset library with any approved assets | Library current |

### Shoot Days (Full day)
- On-set direction: posture, expression, lighting, environment
- Real-time review of shots on monitor or playback
- Call selects at end of shoot day
- Brief editor on color grade direction before leaving

---

## Weekly Responsibilities

### Monday — Asset Review & Planning
- [ ] Review all assets submitted in the last week
- [ ] Approve, request revision, or reject with written notes
- [ ] Load current campaign narrative from Brand Strategy
- [ ] Plan creative output for the week (briefs needed, shoots needed, edits needed)

### Tuesday — Brief Writing
- [ ] Write creative briefs for any upcoming shoots (`workflows/creative-brief.md`)
- [ ] Write photo briefs for product photography needs (`workflows/photo-brief.md`)
- [ ] Write video briefs for any video production this week (`workflows/video-brief.md`)
- [ ] Deliver briefs to photographers/videographers

### Wednesday — Moodboard & Direction
- [ ] Build moodboards for upcoming drops or campaigns (`docs/moodboard-guide.md`)
- [ ] Confirm visual direction is aligned with Brand Strategy's campaign narrative
- [ ] Deliver moodboards to relevant team (TikTok Growth, Paid Ads)

### Thursday — Production Oversight
- [ ] Review in-progress edits — color grade, crop, composition
- [ ] Brief editor on any revisions needed
- [ ] QA all assets scheduled to go live next week

### Friday — Docs & Standards
- [ ] Update `docs/visual-standards.md` if any new visual decisions were made this week
- [ ] Log asset approval rate for the week
- [ ] Plan next week's creative needs (shoots, edits, briefs)

## Example Daily Workflow (Standard Day)

```
08:00  Review submitted assets
       → 3 product photos submitted from Tuesday's shoot
       → Photo 1 (hero flat): APPROVED — light is perfect, product clear
       → Photo 2 (on-body front): REVISION — model's left shoulder slightly dropped,
         reshot needed or correct in post
       → Photo 3 (macro detail): APPROVED — stitching detail excellent
       → Send revision note on Photo 2 with specific direction

08:20  Check TikTok Growth's video going live today
       → View draft: Identity Mirror content, model in concrete space
       → Check: background too bright (concrete in sunlight, not dark room)
       → Flag before posting: "Grade this down — crush the background exposure.
         Current grade reads "lifestyle" not NCD. Specifically: pull blacks to -70 in grade."
       → TikTok Growth revises and resubmits. Approved.

08:45  Answer Paid Ads question: "Can we use the user-generated photo from Marcus?"
       → Review photo: taken on iPhone, good posture, dark background, NCD product clear
       → Decision: Yes — UGC quality meets standard, approve for warm retargeting only
         (not cold — cold needs brand-quality assets)

09:00  Write creative brief for Drop 5 shoot scheduled next week
       → Campaign enemy: "performing instead of building"
       → Model direction: not looking at camera. In motion. Purpose in posture.
       → Environment: empty parking garage (concrete, harsh overhead light)
       → Light: single cold fluorescent source, no fill
       → Deliver brief to photographer

10:00  Done
```

## Example Weekly Workflow (Pre-Drop Week)

```
MON  Asset review: 6 assets submitted. 4 approved, 2 revision.
     Campaign narrative loaded: "Drop 5 — The Standard."
     Plan: creative brief for product shoot + video brief for drop reveal video.

TUE  Write creative brief for Drop 5 shoot:
     Enemy = performing. Model = intentional, not styling for camera.
     Environment = raw industrial. Light = single warm source.
     Deliver to photographer.
     Write video brief for drop reveal: 30-second format, hook = dramatic reveal.

WED  Build Drop 5 moodboard: 6 images.
     1. Atmosphere: abandoned warehouse
     2. Light: single overhead incandescent, deep shadow
     3. Man: Goggins-type frame, purposeful stillness
     4. Space: concrete floor, exposed steel ceiling
     5. Detail: macro of stitching, extreme close
     6. Tone grade: Cormac McCarthy film still (The Road color palette)
     Deliver moodboard to photographer and TikTok Growth.

THU  Review shoot selects from Monday's editorial.
     8 selects from 200 frames. All within brief. Grade direction sent.
     QA Drop 5 video draft. Color grade: good. Rhythm: 1 cut is slow — fix timing.

FRI  All Drop 5 assets approved. Library updated.
     Asset approval rate this week: 7/9 first-pass (78% — just below 80% target).
     Note: lighting brief needs more specific angle direction for next shoot.
     Update visual-standards.md: "Overhead light without fill creates flat ambient —
     always specify key light direction angle in future briefs."
```
