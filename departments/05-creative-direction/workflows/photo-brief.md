# Workflow: Photography Brief
## Department: Creative Direction

Attach this to a completed master creative brief (`creative-brief.md`) for all photo shoot days.

---

## Standard Shot List (per drop)

| Shot # | Description | Format | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero flat — product on black, side-lit | 3:4 + 1:1 | P1 |
| 2 | On-body front — 3/4 turn, intentional posture | 3:4 | P1 |
| 3 | On-body back — shoulder width, hood/seam detail | 3:4 | P1 |
| 4 | Macro detail — fabric weave or stitching | 1:1 | P1 |
| 5 | Environment shot — model in space | 16:9 + 9:16 crop | P2 |
| 6 | Motion shot — mid-movement, fabric in motion | 3:4 | P2 |
| 7 | Layering shot — product worn with other NCD pieces | 3:4 | P3 |
| 8 | Product alone — negative space, minimal | 1:1 | P3 |

---

## Model Direction

**Posture:**
- Shoulders back, slight forward lean — never slouching
- Hands: at rest or in motion — never awkwardly posed
- Eye line: away from camera (first 3 shots) or direct camera contact (closing shots only)
- Expression: neutral to serious — never smiling for camera, never scowling

**What to avoid:**
- Arms crossed (too closed off)
- Looking down at product (breaks authority)
- Over-posed fashion-editorial hands
- Bright expression / performative energy

**Movement direction:**
- Walk toward camera at medium pace — natural, not runway
- Reach for something off-frame
- Turn slowly from back to 3/4 — don't rush

---

## Styling Direction

**NCD piece:** Always the hero. No competing accessories.
**Complementary pieces:** Minimal — dark denim, cargo, clean sweats only
**Footwear:** Simple — black boots, clean sneakers, no logo heavy
**No:** Visible logos from other brands, distracting jewelry, bright accessories

---

## Technical Specs

| Spec | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Resolution | Min 4000px on longest edge |
| Format | RAW + exported JPEG |
| Color profile | sRGB |
| Naming | `NCD-[ProductHandle]-[ShotNum]-[ColorwayCode].jpg` |

---

## Post-Processing Direction (for editor)

- Crush the blacks — shadows to near-zero
- Desaturate midtones — let gold or steel accent carry the warmth
- Skin tones: cool or natural — not warm/sun-kissed
- Sharpen fabric texture in details shots — leave out of hero shots
- Remove all distracting background elements
- Never apply heavy filters or presets that date the image
