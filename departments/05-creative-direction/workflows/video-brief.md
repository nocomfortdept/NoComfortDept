## NCD Video Structure (30-second format)

```
0:00–0:02  ── HOOK
           Visual: [Opening frame — unexpected or textural]
           Text: [On-screen text if used — max 4 words]
           Audio: [Sound hit / ambient / voice line]

0:02–0:08  ── BUILD
           Product in motion or at rest
           Key spec detail shown (not stated)
           No voiceover — let the visual carry

0:08–0:20  ── PROOF
           Construction detail — close ups
           Fit in motion — walking, turning, reaching
           Atmosphere — environment, light

0:20–0:25  ── CLOSE
           Scarcity or brand statement on screen
           Product name + drop number

0:25–0:30  ── BRAND
           Logo hold
           Tagline: "Wear The Discomfort."
           URL or CTA if needed
```

## Filming Direction

**Camera movement:**
- Slow push-ins for product detail — no handheld shake
- Tracking shots: keep horizon level — no tilt or roll
- Static holds for identity shots — longer than feels comfortable
- Fast cuts only for hook or product reveal sequences

**Lens guidance:**
- Wide: environment and model in space (24mm or equivalent)
- Standard: on-body and medium shots (50mm or equivalent)
- Macro: fabric and construction detail (100mm or equivalent)
- Never zoom during a shot — cut between focal lengths

**Audio:**
- Location sound: capture ambient — dark, quiet, industrial is ideal
- Music: dark minimal — no EDM, no pop, no generic workout music
- Voiceover: recorded in clean acoustic space, close-mic, no reverb
- Option: no audio — text only. Often converts better on TikTok.

## Color Grade Direction

Brief for colorist:

```
COLOR GRADE BRIEF
Look: Dark luxury masculine — not cinematic romance, not thriller
Blacks: Crushed — full toe-in, no lifted shadows
Highlights: Protected — retain gold/warm accent in highlight
Midtones: Desaturated — near-monochrome midtones
Skin: Cool or natural — never warm-toned skin
Accent: Gold-warm light source or steel-cold — pick one per video
Reference look: [Describe or link 1 reference frame]
Output: H.264 + ProRes export
```

## Editing Checklist

- [ ] Hook lands in first 2 seconds — no slow fade in
- [ ] No jump cuts in middle of a sentence (voiceover)
- [ ] Music doesn't overpower product sound or ambient
- [ ] Captions/text: Bebas Neue, off-white on dark background
- [ ] Logo appear at end only — never brand-forward at start
- [ ] All cuts intentional — no content just filling time
- [ ] 9:16 crop doesn't cut off product at edges
- [ ] No visible third-party logos or competing brands in frame
